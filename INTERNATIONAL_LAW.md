**Status: CUSTOMARY INTERNATIONAL LAW**
**Scope: WORLDWIDE - ALL NATIONS - ALL PEOPLE**

## 🎯 UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights - 2011

**Status: GLOBALLY RECOGNIZED FRAMEWORK**
**Scope: WORLDWIDE - ALL STATES AND BUSINESSES**

The UN Guiding Principles establish the **"Protect, Respect and Remedy"** framework that applies to **ALL businesses worldwide**, regardless of where they are incorporated or operate.

### Three Pillars:

1. **PILLAR 1: State Duty to Protect**
   - States must protect against human rights abuse by third parties, including business enterprises
   - **Applies to ALL states worldwide**

2. **PILLAR 2: Corporate Responsibility to Respect**
   - Business enterprises should respect human rights
   - **Applies to ALL businesses worldwide**

3. **PILLAR 3: Access to Remedy**
   - States must ensure access to remedy for victims of business-related human rights abuse
   - **Applies to ALL states worldwide**
