# Ethics, Privacy & International Human-Rights Framework

**Project:** NDA Multi-Signal Defensive Protection & Evidence Registry
**Owner:** Ervin Remus Radosavlevici

## 1. Purpose limitation

The Work exists for one purpose: to let an individual keep a private, timestamped, tamper-evident record of technical events they personally observe. It is a **defensive** tool. It is not a surveillance, detection, counter-measure or accusation tool.

## 2. Legal foundations

- **UDHR Art. 12** — no arbitrary interference with privacy.
- **ICCPR Art. 17** — protection of privacy, family, home and correspondence.
- **ECHR Art. 8** — right to respect for private life; interference must be lawful, necessary and proportionate.
- **UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights** — respect, avoid harm, provide remedy.
- **GDPR / data-protection law** (where applicable) — data minimisation, purpose limitation, storage limitation, user rights.

## 3. Principles applied

| Principle                             | How the Work applies it                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Necessity**                         | Only user-typed observations and minimal browser metadata are stored.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Proportionality**                   | No continuous sensor logging; motion, audio, video and location are never captured.                                                                                                                   |
| **Transparency**                      | Every safeguard is stated in the interface, README and this document.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Presumption of innocence**          | The UI instructs users to describe only what was observed and to avoid naming or accusing anyone without independent verification. Classifications are "possible" / "observation", never "confirmed". |
| **Separation of fact and conclusion** | Observation and technical reference are separate fields; no automatic inference is made.                                                                                                              |
| **User autonomy**                     | Users own their data, can export it and can delete it permanently.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Non-interference**                  | No jamming, no hacking, no remote control — of anyone's equipment, ever.                                                                                                                              |
| **Accountability**                    | Records are hashed and timestamped so that later alteration is detectable.                                                                                                                            |

## 4. Prohibited uses

- Targeting, profiling or identifying any individual.
- Publishing records to accuse or defame a person.
- Using exported data as "proof" without independent expert verification.
- Modifying the Work to add covert recording or interference capabilities.

## 5. Guidance for users

1. Record what you saw, heard or measured — not who you think did it.
2. Keep exports in an encrypted location.
3. If you believe you are a victim of stalking or unlawful surveillance, contact law-enforcement, a lawyer, or a recognised victim-support organisation. Bring your exported registry as supporting material, not as a conclusion.
4. Respect the privacy of others while protecting your own.
