## 🛡️ OWNERSHIP & AUTHORSHIP PROTECTION (US CONTEXT)

### Perpetual Protection Mechanisms (US Law)

| Mechanism | Legal Basis | Duration | US Enforcement |
|-----------|-------------|----------|----------------|
| **Copyright** | 17 U.S.C. § 102 | Life + 70 years | Civil & Criminal (17 U.S.C. § 501-506) |
| **Contract Terms** | US Contract Law | Perpetual | Breach of contract |
| **Trademark** | Lanham Act (15 U.S.C.) | Indefinite (renewable) | 15 U.S.C. § 1114, 1125(a) |
| **Timestamp** | WIPO PROOF / Blockchain | Perpetual | Admissible evidence (FRE 901) |
| **DMCA Protection** | 17 U.S.C. § 1201-1203 | Perpetual | Civil & Criminal penalties |

### Three-Layer Protection System (US)

1. **Layer 1: Copyright (17 U.S.C.)**
   - Automatic upon creation under 17 U.S.C. § 102
   - Worldwide protection under Berne Convention
   - Life + 70 years (17 U.S.C. § 302)
   - **US Registration**: Recommended with US Copyright Office for statutory damages

2. **Layer 2: Timestamping (Evidentiary)**
   - WIPO PROOF-style cryptographic hashing
   - Blockchain anchoring
   - **US Admissibility**: Courts admit digital evidence under **FRE 901, 902**
   - See [TIMESTAMP_PROOF_US.md](TIMESTAMP_PROOF_US.md)

3. **Layer 3: Formal Registration (US-Specific)**
   - **US Copyright Office** registration (for statutory damages)
   - **USPTO** trademark registration
   - **State Registrations**: As applicable

**US-SPECIFIC NOTE:** Unlike UK/EU, US law does not recognize perpetual moral rights. Protection relies on **contractual terms** and **trademark law**.
